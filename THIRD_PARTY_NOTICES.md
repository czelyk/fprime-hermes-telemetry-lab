# Third-party provenance

F Prime, Hermes and F Prime GDS are pinned in sources.lock.json. Their license
texts are retained in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/. Source archives are downloaded
anonymously and checksummed; vendor/build/tool caches are ignored by Git.
The generated F Prime dictionary and example-generated local data are included
only to replay the actual recorded host sender stream. They are not mission data.
Python transitive dependencies are pinned in requirements.lock.txt.
